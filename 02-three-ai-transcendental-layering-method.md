# Three-AI Question-First Transcendental Layering Method

**Central principle:** Begin from a secured given; ask for its minimal necessary condition; test that condition by denial and countermodel; classify its exact role; promote it only if it survives; then use it as the given for the next question.

**Current status:** Layers 1 and 2 form the secured foundation. Layers 3 and 4 are the research frontier. Develop and test uncertain layers without presupposing the desired conclusion.

---

## Purpose

Construction method for a layered transcendental philosophy with three independent AI systems. Not merely a debate script. Does not begin with a finished doctrine the AIs are instructed to defend.

Two movements:

1. **Ascending construction:** given → candidate necessary condition.
2. **Descending verification:** return to the original given; test whether the condition was genuinely required.

Aim: determine how far the transcendental sequence legitimately carries — not the strongest available metaphysics.

## Recursive structure

$$G_n \rightarrow Q_n \rightarrow C_n \rightarrow N_n \rightarrow P_n \rightarrow G_{n+1}$$

- $G_n$ secured given  
- $Q_n$ condition-question  
- $C_n$ candidate condition  
- $N_n$ necessity / denial / countermodel tests  
- $P_n$ promotion gate  
- $G_{n+1}$ surviving condition as next given  

**Governing syntax:** each new question begins with **Given that …**

## Epistemic statuses

Secured · Established within the locks · Provisional · Weakened but surviving · Requires a bridge · Branched · Rejected · Open

“Secured” = controlling for the current project, eligible to ground the next question — not immune to all criticism.

---

## Three AI roles

### AI 1 — Phenomenological Grounder

Protects the given. Output: **Given Sheet** (current given, mode, granted / not granted, distinctions, contamination, limits). Rule: **Givenness first, then conditions.**

### AI 2 — Transcendental Constructor

Asks: Given $G_n$, what must already be true for $G_n$ to be possible, presented, or intelligible as the kind of given it is? Proposes the **minimal** candidate. Must not use the desired final doctrine as a hidden selection rule.

### AI 3 — Necessity and Boundary Auditor

Central question: Can the original given remain possible while the proposed condition is denied? Denial, countermodel, minimality, conclusion-grade, category, topology, objectification, modal, scope, doctrinal-contamination tests.

Resolution is **not** majority vote.

---

## Per-layer procedure (compact)

1. State one current given.  
2. Classify mode of givenness.  
3. AI 1 → Given Sheet.  
4. Givenness gate.  
5. One condition-question (**Given that …** — question must not contain the answer).  
6. AI 2 → minimal candidate; classify type (constitutive / causal / explanatory / interpretive / symbolic).  
7. AI 3 denial test.  
8. AI 3 strongest countermodel.  
9. Minimal surviving conclusion (strip unproved additions).  
10. Topology test (vertical condition vs grade vs field vs reflection vs symbol vs branch).  
11. Controlled cross-review (proposition-specific).  
12. Promotion gate.  
13. Next nested question.  
14. Repeat.  
15. Stopping rule when necessity fails, bridge needed, doctrine only, object-shift, overreach, or genuine open question.

Stopping is not failure — it marks the frontier.
