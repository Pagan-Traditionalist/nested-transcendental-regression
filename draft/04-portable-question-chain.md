# Portable Question Chain — Sword Diagnostic

**Object:** sword (“the sword is shiny”) — never knife.  
**Ideal debate line:** Steps **1–2** secured. Step **3** optional. Step **4** off-line unless topic shifted.  
**Syntax:** each new question begins with **Given that …**

---

## Memorize (main line)

**0. Bait (optional)**  
If the sword is shiny → reflective; the sword is shiny; therefore reflective.  
*(Do not fight validity. Ambush.)*

**1. [SECURED]**  
What are the necessary conditions for “the sword is shiny” to be an objective representation of empirical reality at all?  
→ **Subject ↔ Object** under **Space, Time, Causality**.

**2. [SECURED]**  
**Given that** structure, what must be true of whatever functions as subject in that act?  
→ Subject-pole = **nonobjectified role** of *that* act (for whom the object appears) — **role distinction**, not numerical dualism / eternal soul.

**STOP here** for ideal sword diagnostic.

## Optional (only if forced)

**3. [PROVISIONAL — only if they identify subject with ordinary empirical me]**  
**Given that** the subject-role is not the object of that same act, what must be true for the empirical “me” itself to appear as an object?  
→ Appearing empirical ego does **not exhaust** the subject-pole of the act in which it appears.

**4. [SPECULATIVE — off this line unless topic shifted]**  
**Given that** sword and empirical ego can both be given within consciousness, what must be true for that consciousness to be present?  
→ At least some presence does **not** wait on a further objectifying act (negative anti-regress only).
