## What was refined (Steps 1–2 remain Secured)

### Step 1

**Forced link:** From P2 as *objective empirical representation* (not as true proposition, not as justified belief) to Subject ↔ Object under Space, Time, Causality.

**Why forced:** Without a for-whom / of-what structure, and without space–time–causal conditions of empirical objecthood, “the sword is shiny” collapses into sentence, concept-play, or unordered sensation.

**Wording refine:** Keep causality thin — *referral of affection to an object*, not ontological Will-force. Do not overload the category table.

**Not forced:** realism adjudication; full Kantianism; any soul claim.

### Step 2

**Forced link:** From that structure to the **role** of the subject-pole in *that particular act*: that for whom the object appears must not, in the same respect, be the object of that act.

**Why forced:** If the “subject” is only another object *in that same act-respect*, the “to whom” disappears. Reflection and self-models move the boundary; they do not abolish it.

**Wording refine (clarity lock):** Emphasize **ROLE distinction** / “of that particular act.” Explicitly deny that Step 2 asserts numerical dualism or eternal soul. That denial is part of the secured content’s *limit*, not a later hedge.

**Not forced:** substance dualism; *sākṣin* ontology; aesthetic correlativity.

## Topology

```
CHAIN (vertical nest):     Entry → 1 → 2  [secured]
                           2 → 3 → 4      [frontier; optional]

BRANCH (optional exits):   from 2 (or after 3) → A aesthetic grade
                           from method orientation → B OpenTI/Will

FIELD (invariant):         sākṣin-as-subject-field may be *named* across grades
                           — never inserted as third object-rung
                           (particular sword → archetypal sword → ✗ witness-object)

GRADE (not rung):          empirical presentation ≠ archetypal/aesthetic presentation
                           of the *same* sword
```

**Chain vs nest:** The debate script is a *nest* (each answer is the *given* for the next question via **Given that …**). It is not a flat list of independent theses.

**Why BRANCH A is not Step 5:** “The eternal knows the eternal” changes the *mode/grade* of presentation. Treating it as another deduction from empirical P2 inflates the nest and mixes tracks.

**Why *sākṣin* is field, not rung:** A third object after particular→archetype reifies the witness and restarts Step 2’s problem (objectifying the pole). Field language keeps invariance without object-stacking.
