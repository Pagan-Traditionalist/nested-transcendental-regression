## Step 1 — Objective Representation — Secured

**Q:** What must already be the case for P2 — “the sword is shiny” — to be an objective representation of empirical reality at all?

**A:** Subject ↔ Object under **Space**, **Time**, and **Causality** — where Causality means affection **referred** to an empirical object as its cause (object vs unordered sensation). Not laboratory etiology; not Will-force; not determinism slogan.

**Grade:** Constitutive of objective empirical *purport*, not mind-independent realism-proof; not BIV-defeat.

**Follow-up if proposition-first:** Not justification. What must already be the case for there to be an empirical sword represented as shiny.

## Step 2 — Subject Qua Subject (role) — Secured — MAIN-LINE STOP

**Q (full):** Given that objective empirical representation requires a subject–object structure in a presenting act, what must be true of the *role* occupied by the subject-pole in *that same act* — such that the object appears *to* it — without treating that pole, *in that act and respect*, as one more object among the act’s objects?

**Q (card):** Given S↔O under STC for the sword — in *that same act and respect*, what must hold of the subject-*role* so the object appears *to* it without that role counting as one more object of the act?

**A:** Whatever occupies the subject-role in a given act is not, in that same act and respect, the object of that act. Reflection relocates the boundary; it does not abolish it. **No numerical dualism.**

**MAIN-LINE STOP HERE** for the ideal sword diagnostic.
