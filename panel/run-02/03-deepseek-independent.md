## 2. Step 2 wording traps — Denial + variants

**Candidate:** Role-minimal subject-pole; reflection relocates.

### Denials

1. Self-representing systems / HOT make “nonobjectified pole” empty.  
2. Reflection absorbs subject.  
3. Subject = organism already.  
4. No-act eliminativism.  
5. Soft wording invites Pure Subject as “what must be true of the subject-pole.”

### Strongest countermodel

Reflexive self-model: objectifying the self-model allegedly shows identity, not a further pole.

### Auditor reply

Shows something that *can* occupy subject-role can later be objectified. Does **not** show that in the act where the sword appears as object, the subject-pole of *that* act is object of *that same* act.

### Exact surviving minimal conclusion

> Role-minimal: Whatever functions as subject-pole of a presenting act is not, in that same act and respect, the object of that act. Reflection relocates. **Not** numerical dualism. **Not** Pure Subject necessity.

### Variant audit

| Variant | Verdict |
|---------|---------|
| Appearing-to only (soft) | Survive but substance-invite risk |
| Role + act/respect + without-clause | **Strongest portable** |
| “Is subject numerically ≠ organism?” | **Kill as Step 2 question** |
| “What Pure Subject exists?” | **Kill** |

**Recommended Step 2 Q (critic):**

> **Given that** objective empirical representation requires a subject–object structure in a presenting act, **what must be true of the *role* occupied by the subject-pole in *that same act*** — such that the object appears *to* it — **without** treating that pole, *in that act and respect*, as one more object among the act’s objects?

**Recommended A:** Whatever occupies the subject-role in a given act is not, **in that same act and respect**, the object of that act. Reflection relocates; does not abolish. No numerical dualism.

**Promotion gate L2:** PASS (secured) at role-grade.
