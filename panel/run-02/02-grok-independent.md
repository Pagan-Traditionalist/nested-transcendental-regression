## Step 1 — Objective Representation (Causality wording refined)

**Status:** Secured

**Exact question**

> What must already be the case for P2 — “the sword is shiny” — to be an objective representation of empirical reality at all?

**Refined follow-up**

> Not: what proposition justifies it. What must already be the case for there to be an empirical sword represented as shiny.

**Minimal expected answer**

**Subject ↔ Object**, with the empirical object under:

- **Space** — somewhere / extended / distinct  
- **Time** — now / before / after; persistence and change  
- **Causality (portable wording — candidate A):** affection is **referred** to an empirical object as its cause — so there is an *object* rather than unordered sensation. **Not** laboratory etiology; **not** Will-force; **not** determinism slogan.

**Alternate Causality candidates (constructor ranked)**

1. **A (preferred):** referral of affection → object-as-cause-of-affection (object vs sensation).  
2. **B:** minimal causal order in empirical purport (weaker; vaguer).  
3. **C:** “determining ground of perception” (scholar-heavy; demote for debate card).

**Denial test**

Deny S↔O or deny Space/Time/Causality-as-referral: can P2 remain objective *empirical* representation?

**Strongest countermodel (constructor pressure)**

- Concept-play with no world-directed purport.  
- Sensation-bundle without object-reference.  
- Skeptical scenes that still *import* STC into represented content.  
- Physics-first hijack: “causality means photons → retina” — wrong explanatory level; relocate, don’t cancel Step 1.

**What NOT established**

Truth of P2; realism vs idealism; full categories; Will; soul; field; aesthetic claim.

**Bridge to Step 2**

Surviving given: objective empirical representation of the sword requires subject representing object under Space, Time, and **referral-minimal Causality**.

## Step 2 — Subject Qua Subject (role-indexed variants)

**Status:** Secured

### Candidate questions (constructor offers; merge will pick)

**Q-α (appearing-to primary)**

> **Given that** objective empirical representation requires a subject–object structure under space, time, and causality, **what must be true of the subject-pole such that the object appears *to* it, rather than that same pole appearing as an object in that same act?**

**Q-β (role-indexed primary — preferred)**

> **Given that** objective empirical representation requires a subject–object structure in a presenting act, **what must be true of the *role* occupied by the subject-pole in *that same act*** — such that the object appears *to* it — **without** treating that pole, *in that act and respect*, as one more object among the act’s objects?

**Q-γ (compressed debate card)**

> **Given** S↔O under STC for the sword — in *that same act and respect*, what must hold of the subject-*role* so the object appears *to* it without that role counting as one more object of the act?

**Constructor pick for nest body:** **Q-β** (full); **Q-γ** as portable card line.

**Minimal expected answer**

In *that particular act*, the subject-pole must:

1. be that **for whom** the object appears, and  
2. remain the **nonobjectified pole** of that act.

**Role, not entity-count.** Reflection **relocates** the boundary; it does **not** abolish it. **No numerical dualism.**

**Denial test**

In one and the same respect of one and the same act, identify subject-pole with an object of that act: does “appears *to*” survive?

**Strongest countermodel**

- Reflection (“I see myself”) — boundary move.  
- Cameras / mirrors / self-models as objects within representation.  
- “Subject = brain” as role-identity in the same act — collapses; may open optional Step 3 if interlocutor insists empirical-me ID.

**What NOT established**

Two substances; immortality; Pure Subject; *sākṣin* as third rung; aesthetic correlativity.

**Ideal debate main-line: STOP after Step 2.**
