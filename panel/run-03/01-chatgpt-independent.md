## 2) Given Sheet — Step 3 (FOCUS)

### Trigger given (G₃ candidate)

**Only if** interlocutor identifies the subject-pole with the ordinary empirical “me” (this body, this biography, this personality, this brain-as-person).

Without that identification move, Step 3 is **not** a forced nest link from Step 2.

### Mode of givenness

| Aspect | Classification |
|---|---|
| Mode | Identification-challenge / objectifiability bridge — not automatic deduction |
| Orientation | Empirical me *as appearing content* vs subject-role of the act of its appearing |
| Status under locks | **Provisional / Branch** — not Secured main-line |

### What a Step 3 Given Sheet may grant (minimal)

1. Body, memory, desire, emotion, personality, biography, willing **can** appear as empirical object-content.  
2. When they so appear, they occupy **object-role** of *that* appearing act.  
3. Therefore, under the description “appearing empirical me,” they do not **exhaust** the subject-role of the act in which they appear.  
4. This is a **grade of non-exhaustion under a description**, not a census of substances.

### What must not be granted as necessity

1. A Pure Subject / soul / witness-entity **numerically** other than the organism.  
2. Schopenhauer’s aesthetic Pure Subject of Knowing as required by P2.  
3. *Sākṣin* as third object-rung.  
4. Automatic warrant for Step 4.  
5. “There are two selves” as transcendental necessity.

### Objectifiability bridge — phenomenological articulation

**Bridge (1):** If X appears as empirical object-content in act A, then X *qua that appearing content* is on the object-side of A.  
**Bridge (2) careful:** From (1) you get: appearing-determinable empirical ego ≠ subject-role of the act of its appearing.  
**Bridge (2) illicit:** From (1) you get a Pure Subject numerically distinct from the person.

Research note: interlocutors often hear (2 careful) as (2 illicit). Contamination control is the whole game.
