### Bridge (explicit)

**Objectifiability bridge:** If the empirical me can appear as object-content, then *qua that appearing content* it is object-side in that act; therefore it does not, under that description, occupy the subject-role of that act.

**Non-exhaustion grade (surviving):** Empirical ego as appearing content does not exhaust the subject-pole of the act in which that content appears.

**Numerical dualism (killed):** No claim that a Pure Subject / soul exists as a second entity necessary from P2 or from this bridge.

### Exact question

> **Given that** the subject-role in an act is not the object of that same act, **and given that** you identify that subject-role with the ordinary empirical “me,” **what must be true for that empirical “me” itself to appear as an object of experience?**

### Minimal expected answer (weakened)

Body, memory, desire, emotion, personality, biography can appear as objects. Appearing presupposes a subject-pole for whom they appear.

> The empirical ego **as appearing content** does not *exhaust* the subject-pole that conditions its appearing.  
> Hold **role/exhaustion grade only** — **not** Pure Subject / soul / numerical nonidentity of substances.

### Denial test

Claim the appearing, determinable empirical ego fully exhausts subjectivity: can it still *appear* with no residual for-whom under that description?
