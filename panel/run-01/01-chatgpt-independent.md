## 1) Given Sheet — Step 1

### Current given (G₁)

The working given is not the syllogism’s validity and not the truth of “the sword is shiny” as a contested empirical claim. It is the **analytic grant** that P2 can be treated as an **objective representation of empirical reality** — i.e., as world-directed presentation of an empirical sword as shiny — for the purpose of asking what that already presupposes.

Syllogism (diagnostic only):

- If the sword is shiny, then the sword is reflective.  
- The sword is shiny.  
- Therefore, the sword is reflective.

The syllogism is a **staging device**. It is not the given to be justified propositionally.

### Mode of givenness

| Aspect | Classification |
|---|---|
| Mode | **Granted-for-analysis** of objective empirical purport (not sense-data inventory; not mere sentence-token; not yet a metaphysical claim about things-in-themselves) |
| Orientation | Intuition-before-propositions: the given is the *world-directed empirical presentation*, not the next supporting proposition |
| Status under locks | **Secured** (working assumption; wording may be refined; reopen only for material defect needing owner amendment) |

### What is granted

1. That “the sword is shiny” can be taken as purporting to present an **empirical object** (the sword) with an **empirical determination** (shiny).  
2. That this purport is **objective** in the minimal sense: not merely a free-floating conceptual string, not merely private ineffable feel without object-reference.  
3. That the transcendental question is legitimate: *What must already be the case for that objective empirical representation to be possible / intelligible as such?*  
4. That Subject ↔ Object under Space, Time, Causality is the **secured core answer** for this project’s Step 1 (refine wording; do not invent a rival secured core on this pass).

### What is not granted (at Step 1)

1. That the sword exists independently of all representation (noumenal realism not required here).  
2. That P2 is true, justified, or perceptually verified (truth/justification are deliberately set aside).  
3. That “subject” already means soul, Pure Subject, *ātman*, transcendental ego as substance, or a numerically distinct entity from body/brain.  
4. That Space / Time / Causality are already fully Kantian forms, Schopenhauerian *principium rationis*, or scientific spacetime laws — only their **role as transcendental conditions of objective empirical objecthood** is secured as core.  
5. That later nest steps (empirical ego, prereflective presence, *sākṣin*, aesthetic correlativity) are already implied by Step 1.

### Distinctions Step 1 must keep sharp

- **Proposition** vs **objective empirical purport of that proposition**  
- **Justification of P2** vs **conditions that make P2 an objective empirical representation at all**  
- **Horizontal regress** (proposition → argument → definition → …) vs **transcendental interrupt** (change of explanatory level)  
- **What appears** vs **what makes its appearing as empirical object possible**  
- **Sensory affection / unordered sensation** vs **object as cause-referred empirical unity** (causality’s role here is transcendental referral, not a completed physics)

### Minimal secured content (Step 1 core — refine, don’t replace)

For “the sword is shiny” to be an objective representation of empirical reality, there must already be:

1. **Subject ↔ Object** correlation (something appearing *as* object *to* a subject-pole).  
2. **Space** — somewhere / extended / here–there / distinct from other objects.  
3. **Time** — now / before / after; persistence and change.  
4. **Causality** — sensory affection referred to an empirical object as its cause, so there is an *object* rather than merely unordered sensation.

### Contamination risks at Step 1 (preview; expanded in §4)

- Proposition-first capture: answering with another belief, definition, or justification instead of conditions of representation.  
- Overloading **causality** into full scientific determinism or metaphysical Will.  
- Treating the syllogism’s formal validity as the philosophical issue.  
- Smuggling a soul or Pure Subject into “subject” already at Step 1.  
- Equating “objective” with “mind-independent in every sense” or with “public language game only.”

### Limits of this Given Sheet

This sheet **protects G₁** and records the secured Step 1 core. It does **not** promote Step 3 or Step 4. It does **not** say how far nesting may go.

## 2) Given Sheet — Step 2

### Current given (G₂)

**Given that** objective empirical representation requires Subject ↔ Object under Space, Time, Causality:

What must be true of the **subject-pole** such that the object appears *to* it, rather than the subject-pole itself appearing as the object **in the same act**?

### Mode of givenness

| Aspect | Classification |
|---|---|
| Mode | **Role-structural given** derived from the secured Step 1 structure (not a new empirical observation; not a substance claim) |
| Orientation | Condition-question about the *pole of appearing-to*, not about a second empirical object alongside the sword |
| Status under locks | **Secured core** — role distinction; refine wording; **not** numerical dualism / soul / Pure Subject as necessary from P2 |

### What is granted

1. That within a given representational act, there is a **subject-role** and an **object-role**.  
2. That the subject-pole is **that for whom** the object appears.  
3. That in **that particular act**, the subject-pole remains the **nonobjectified pole** of the act.  
4. That **reflection moves the boundary** (the former subject-content can become object) but **does not abolish** the need for a subject-pole of the new act.  
5. That “of that particular act” blocks premature leap to two substances or a permanent Pure Subject deduced from P2 alone.

### What is not granted (at Step 2)

1. Numerical dualism: two entities, soul vs body, Pure Subject vs empirical person, as *necessary* from Step 2.  
2. That the subject-pole is already proven **not** identical with the ordinary empirical me in every respect (that is Step 3 territory / frontier — only opened if interlocutor collapses subject into ordinary empirical me).  
3. That *sākṣin* / invariant field is already established (locks: *sākṣin* = invariant field, not a third object-rung; not deduced here).  
4. Aesthetic correlativity (“the eternal knows the eternal”) as a fifth deduction from P2 (locks: separate grade/track).  
5. Any HVHR / reincarnation / fitra / Nietzsche content (locked out).

### Distinctions Step 2 must keep sharp

- **Role distinction** vs **numerical nonidentity**  
- **Subject-pole of act A** vs **empirical self as possible object in act B**  
- **Reflection objectifies a prior subject-content** vs **reflection eliminates subjectivity**  
- **Nonobjectified pole** vs **hidden object waiting to be discovered**  
- **“Whatever functions as subject in that act”** vs **“there exists a Pure Subject of Knowing”**

### Minimal secured content (Step 2 core — refine wording)

In that particular act, the subject must:

1. be **that for whom** the object appears, and  
2. remain the **nonobjectified pole** of that act.

Working conclusion form (locks-aligned):

> Whatever occupies the subject-role in a given act is not, in that same respect, the object of that act. The moment it is made an object, another subject-pole is already presupposed. Reflection moves the subject–object boundary; it never abolishes it.

### Contamination risks at Step 2 (preview; expanded in §4)

- Soul-smuggling: converting role asymmetry into a spiritual substance.  
- Brain-as-subject confusion: either (a) flatly identifying the subject-pole with the brain *as appearing object*, or (b) treating “brain causes experience” as already answering the role question.  
- Treating Step 2 as already proving Pure Subject / Layer 3–4 metaphysics.  
- Proposition-first capture: demanding a definition of “subject” that restarts conceptual regress instead of holding the role structure.

### Limits of this Given Sheet

Protects G₂ as **role** structure. Does **not** decide whether the nest should stop after Step 2, branch to empirical-ego analysis, or continue. No Stop / Branch / Promote verdict.

### 3.3 Rival readings of Space / Time / Causality as transcendental conditions

| Condition | Minimal transcendental role (secured) | Rival over-readings to resist | Rival under-readings to resist |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Space** | Condition for here/there, extension, object-distinctness | Absolute Newtonian container; full GR metric as already proven | Purely linguistic “spatial talk”; merely imagined location |

### 3.4 Cross-cutting rival frames for the whole Step 1–2 package

1. **Kantian formal** — conditions of possible experience; careful with how much Critique baggage is imported.  
2. **Schopenhauerian representational** — world as representation; careful not to smuggle Will or aesthetic Pure Subject into Steps 1–2.  
3. **Phenomenological (Husserlian/Sartrean)** — act intentionality, non-positional self-awareness; useful for wording Step 2; risk of jumping to prereflective presence (Step 4) too early.  
4. **Analytic representationalism / content theories** — can support “objective purport” but often stay proposition-first.  
5. **Eliminativist / illusionist** — deny subject-pole or phenomenal appearing-to; relevant as denial/countermodel material for the critic, not as a Given Sheet rewrite.  
6. **Enactivist / embodied** — subject as skilled organism; may blur role vs body-as-object; needs careful separation.

## 4) Contamination risks (explicit kill-watch for constructor/critic)

### 4.1 Soul-smuggling

**Pattern:** “Nonobjectified pole” → “therefore an immaterial soul / Pure Subject / immortal witness exists.”  
**Why toxic here:** Step 2 secures **role asymmetry in an act**, not a substance inventory.  
**Detection:** Any conclusion that adds endurance, immortality, simplicity, or spiritual essence without a new given and a new necessity test.  
**Grounder protection:** Keep “of that particular act”; forbid numerical dualism as *necessary from P2*.

### 4.2 Proposition-first capture

**Pattern:** Interlocutor (or AI constructor) answers the transcendental question with another proposition, definition, evidence chain, or conceptual analysis loop.  
**Why toxic:** Restores the horizontal regress the method interrupts.  
**Detection:** Replies that never leave “what justifies / what do you mean / define subject.”  
**Grounder protection:** Reassert: grant P2 for analysis; ask for **conditions of objective empirical representation**, not further propositional support.

### 4.3 Brain-as-subject confusion

**Pattern A:** “The subject is the brain” — while the brain is simultaneously treated as an empirical object in space/time/causality.  
**Pattern B:** Causal story (“neural processing produces the shiny-sword experience”) offered as if it answered the subject-pole **role** question.  
**Why toxic:** Conflates **empirical object in the representation** with **pole for whom representation appears**; may beg the transcendental structure it claims to replace.  
**Detection:** Third-person brain talk that never accounts for appearing-*to*; identity claims that ignore act-relative nonobjectification.  
**Grounder protection:** Distinguishing “brain as appearing/determinable object” from “subject-pole of the act” is allowed as a *distinction of respect*; claiming full numerical metaphysics is not yet granted.

### 4.4 Adjacent contaminations (keep on watchlist)

| Risk | Symptom |
|---|---|
| **Causality overload** | Step 1 becomes determinism, physics fundamentalism, or Will metaphysics |
| **Aesthetic shortcut** | “Eternal knows eternal” inserted as deduction from P2 |
| **Sākṣin objectification** | Witness turned into a third thing alongside sword and ego |
| **Layer desire** | Promoting Step 3/4 because a later doctrine is wanted |
| **Object-shift** | Changing the question from sword-representation to cosmology/theology mid-nest |
| **Status inflation** | Calling Speculative material “Secured” |
| **Locked-out doctrines** | HVHR / reincarnation / fitra / Nietzsche content entering as “conditions” |
