## Step 1 — Objective Representation

**Status:** Secured

**Exact question**

> What must already be the case for P2 — “the sword is shiny” — to be an objective representation of empirical reality at all?

**Refined follow-up** (if they answer with belief, evidence, definition, or further proposition):

> Not: what proposition justifies it. What must already be the case for there to be an empirical sword represented as shiny.

**Minimal expected answer**

**Subject ↔ Object**, with the empirical object under:

- **Space** — somewhere / extended / distinct  
- **Time** — now / before / after; persistence and change  
- **Causality** — affection referred to an empirical object as its cause (object vs unordered sensation)

**Denial test**

Deny subject–object correlation, or deny space / time / causality as conditions of empirical objectivity: can P2 remain an *objective empirical* representation?

**Strongest countermodel (constructor’s own pressure)**

- Sentence / concept-play with no world-directed purport.  
- Sensation-bundle without object-reference.  
- Skeptical scenes that still *import* STC into the represented content (relocate Step 1; do not cancel it).

**What NOT established**

Truth of P2; realism vs idealism; full category table; causality as ontological Will-force; any soul / field / aesthetic claim.

**Bridge to Step 2**

Surviving given: objective empirical representation of the sword requires subject representing object under STC.

**Next question**

> **Given that** objective empirical representation of the sword requires a subject representing an object under space, time, and causality, **what must be true of whatever functions as subject in that act?**

## Step 2 — Subject Qua Subject (role distinction) — wording refined

**Status:** Secured

**Exact question (refined)**

> **Given that** objective empirical representation requires a subject–object structure under space, time, and causality, **what must be true of the subject-pole such that the object appears *to* it, rather than that same pole appearing as an object in that same act?**

**Minimal expected answer (refined)**

In *that particular act*, the subject-pole must:

1. be that **for whom** the object appears, and  
2. remain the **nonobjectified pole** of that act.

**Role, not entity-count:** distinction of *functions within the act* — not numerical dualism, not eternal soul, not Pure Subject as necessary from P2.

**Reflection clause (secured limit):** If reflection objectifies an empirical self, that reflected self is now object; a subject-pole is still presupposed for the reflective act. Reflection **moves** the subject–object boundary; it does **not** abolish it.

**Denial test**

In one and the same respect of one and the same act, identify the subject-pole with an object of that act: does “appears *to*” survive?

**Strongest countermodel**

- “I see myself” (reflection) — boundary move, not abolition.  
- Cameras / mirrors / self-models as objects *within* representation — do not cancel for-whom.  
- “Subject = brain” said as *role*-identity in the same act — collapses appearing organ into pole-for-which (opens optional Step 3 if interlocutor insists).

**What NOT established**

Two substances; immortality; Pure Subject of Knowing; *sākṣin* as proved entity or third object-rung; aesthetic correlativity.

**Bridge**

Surviving given: whatever occupies the subject-role in a given act is not, in that same respect, the object of that act.

**Ideal debate main-line: STOP after Step 2.**

**Optional next question** (only if interlocutor identifies subject-pole with ordinary empirical me):

> **Given that** the subject-role in an act is not the object of that same act, **is this subject-pole simply the ordinary empirical “me”?**

## Step 3 — Empirical Ego (optional extension)

**Status:** Provisional  
**Debate gate:** Enter only if interlocutor identifies subject with ordinary empirical me.  
**Bridge type:** identification-challenge, not automatic nest-force from Step 2 alone.

**Exact question**

> **Given that** the subject-pole is not the object *qua* object in that act, **what must be true for the empirical “me” itself to appear as an object of experience?**

**Minimal expected answer (weakened)**

Body, memory, desire, emotion, personality, biography, willing *can* appear as objects. Appearing presupposes a subject-pole for whom they appear.

> The empirical ego **as appearing content** does not *exhaust* the subject-pole that conditions its appearing.

Thin hold: brain/body/empirical person *as appearing object* ≠ subject-pole *of the act in which they appear*. **Do not** claim numerical nonidentity of substances.

**Denial test**

Claim the appearing, determinable empirical ego fully exhausts subjectivity: can it still *appear* with no residual for-whom?

**Strongest countermodel**

Self-representing / higher-order monitoring systems; identity theories that equate mind with brain-*as-object* while retaining unexplained first-person givenness; “no subject, only processes” that still smuggle appearing-as-such.

**What NOT established**

Pure Subject as necessary; aesthetic Will-suspension from P2; *sākṣin* doctrine; automatic warrant for Step 4.

**Promotion rule:** Do not promote Step 3 because Step 4 is desired.

**Optional next**

> **Given that** both empirical sword and empirical ego can be given within consciousness, **what must be true of that consciousness for this givenness to be consciously present?**

## Step 4 — Nonobjectifying Presence (off main line)

**Status:** Speculative  
**Debate gate:** Off ideal sword line unless topic shifts to presence / objectification regress.  
**Bridge type:** anti-regress of mediation — negative result preferred over positive metaphysics.

**Exact question**

> **Given that** empirical objects and the empirical ego can be given within consciousness, **what must be true for that consciousness itself to be present without endless further objectification?**

**Minimal expected answer (negative first)**

If presence of C₁ required further conscious objectifying act C₂ ad infinitum, presence never arrives.

> At least some conscious presence cannot wait on a further objectifying act (prereflective self-manifestness in the **limited anti-regress** sense only).

Force: objectifying mediation cannot be the *ground* of conscious presence. Do **not** uniquely force self-luminous-field ontology over rival first-order labels.

**Denial test**

Require every conscious presence to be present only as object for another consciousness: does the regress terminate in actual presence?

**Strongest countermodel**

Unconscious higher-order stops that *owe* givenness; functional monitoring without phenomenology (subject-matter shift); rival first-order theories that grant the negative under another name.

**What NOT established**

Unique field metaphysics; *sākṣin* proof; aesthetic eternal-knows-eternal; OpenTI/Will; any “fifth deduction” from P2.

**Stop reason:** further promotion needs a bridge the empirical nest does not force.
