## 1. Step 1 — Denial + strongest countermodels

**Candidate under test:** Objective empirical representation of P2 requires Subject ↔ Object under Space, Time, Causality (STC).

### Denials

1. **Brain-in-vat / global illusion:** There is no external sword; only neural states. Therefore no “objective empirical” structure is required — only internal processing.
2. **Linguistic / deflationary:** “The sword is shiny” is a sentence with assertoric force and truth-conditions in a language-game; “representation of empirical reality” is optional metaphysics.
3. **Coherence / holistic:** Objectivity = mutual coherence of a belief web; no need for a subject–object *act* structure or STC as transcendental forms.
4. **Eliminative / purely causal story:** Talk of “appearing to a subject” is folk psychology; only causal covariation of organism and environment matters.
5. **Phenomenalist collapse:** There are only sensations/appearances; “object” is a convenient fiction — so Subject↔Object is overbuilt.

### Strongest countermodel (pressure)

**Global BIV + linguistic assertibility:** An envatted system produces token “the sword is shiny” with internal coherence and linguistic norms, no outer space-time object, and no causal “affection from an empirical sword.” If Step 1 claimed *ontic* outer-world realism, this kills it.

### Does Step 1 survive?

**Yes — but only at the grade of transcendental condition of *objective empirical purport*, not of metaphysical realism about swords.**

Refine the secured claim:

> *If* a token is to count as an **objective empirical representation** (world-directed purport of an empirical object under shared public-space/time/causal order, as opposed to mere sensation, dream-report, or free-floating sentence), *then* the correlative structure Subject↔Object under Space, Time, and (minimal) Causality is already presupposed *in the sense of that purport*.

**Survives as:** constitutive condition of the *kind* of given Step 1 asks about — objective empirical purport — not as proof that a mind-independent sword exists, not as full Kantian table of categories, not as a thick causal theory of perception.

**Strip from Step 1:**
- Proof against BIV / skepticism (script already says: do not fight “why not a brain?” here)
- Overloading Causality into full scientific etiology
- Treating STC as the only possible forms in every possible mind (scope: for *our* empirical objectivity as the script uses it)

**Promotion gate L1:** **PASS (secured)** — as purport-condition, not realism-proof.

## 2. Step 2 — Denial + countermodels; minimal surviving conclusion

**Candidate under test:** In the act that presents the sword as object, the subject-pole is that *for whom* the object appears; it is not, *in that same act/respect*, appearing-as-object. Reflection moves the boundary; it does not abolish it. Role ≠ numerical dualism.

### Denials

1. **Self-representing systems:** A system can represent itself (mirror, self-model, HOT); therefore “subject not object in the same act” is false or empty.
2. **Reflection absorbs subject:** When I reflect, I *am* the object I take myself to be; no leftover pole.
3. **Subject = organism:** The subject is just the biological organism / brain / empirical person — already an object in the world — so the “nonobjectified pole” is a grammatical illusion.
4. **No-act eliminativism:** There is no “act of appearing”; only information processing. Role talk is reified phenomenology.
5. **Identity theory shortcut:** Subject-pole *is* identical to some brain state that can also be an object of another representation; hence no irreducible role.

### Strongest countermodel

**Reflexive self-model (robot / HOT / predictive self):** At t₁ the system token-represents the sword; at t₂ it token-represents “I-am-seeing-sword,” with the self-model as object-content. The denier says: the subject *was* that model all along; objectifying it shows identity, not a further pole.

### Auditor reply (finite)

The countermodel shows that **something** that can occupy the subject-role can *later* or *in another respect* be objectified. It does **not** show that, *in the very act* in which the sword appears as object, the subject-pole of *that* act is itself given as object of *that same* act. A self-model that appears is appearing-to…; the boundary moved. “Subject = organism” either (a) names the organism *as appearing object* (then the pole-for-whom remains), or (b) stipulates numerical identity by theory — which is not established by Step 2 and must not be smuggled in as transcendental necessity.

### Exact surviving minimal conclusion

> **Role-minimal:** Whatever functions as the subject-pole of a given presenting act is not, in that same act and respect, the object of that act. Objectifying what previously occupied the subject-role presupposes a (possibly relocated) subject-pole for the objectifying act. This is a **role / respect** claim, **not** numerical dualism, **not** immortality of a Pure Subject, **not** proof that the organism is not the realizer.

**Promotion gate L2:** **PASS (secured)** — at role-grade only.

## 3. Step 3 — Why “empirical ego non-exhaustion” needs a bridge

**Target claim (frontier):** The empirical “me” as appearing content does not exhaust the subject-pole presupposed in its appearing.

### Why a bridge is required

Step 2 only yields: subject-role ≠ object-role *in the same act*.  
Step 3 wants: empirical ego (body, memory, personality, biography…) *as appearing* does not **exhaust** the subject-pole.

That is a **stronger** claim. It needs an explicit bridge, e.g.:

1. **Objectifiability bridge:** If X can appear as empirical object-content, then X *qua that appearing content* is on the object-side of the act in which it so appears; therefore X *qua appearing content* does not occupy the subject-role of that act.
2. **Exhaustion bridge (further):** From (1) alone you do **not** get “there exists a Pure Subject numerically distinct from the person.” You get only: **appearing-determinable empirical ego ≠ subject-role of the act of its appearing.**

Without (1)+(careful)(2), promoting Step 3 conflates **objectifiability** with **numerical nonidentity**.

### What grade survives without numerical claim

> **Grade that may survive (provisional, branched):** The empirical ego *as empirically appearing and determinable content* does not, under that description, exhaust the subject-pole of the act in which that content appears.  
> **Does not survive as necessity:** A Pure Subject / soul / witness-entity numerically other than the organism; Schopenhauer’s aesthetic Pure Subject of Knowing as *required* by P2; *sākṣin* as third rung.

**Promotion gate L3:** **HOLD — Requires bridge; branch only if interlocutor identifies subject with ordinary empirical me.** Ideal sword line does **not** promote L3 by default.

## 4. Step 4 — Anti-regress ≠ self-manifest field metaphysics

**Target claim (frontier / off ideal line):** If presence of C₁ required further objectifying C₂ ad infinitum, presence would never obtain; therefore some presence is nonobjectifying / prereflective (anti-regress).

### Why this ≠ self-manifest field metaphysics

| Anti-regress (negative) | Field metaphysics (positive) |
|-------------------------|------------------------------|
| Objectifying mediation cannot be the *sole* ground of conscious presence | Consciousness *is* a self-luminous / self-manifest field |
| Compatible with several labels (intrinsic first-order consciousness, prereflective self-awareness, etc.) | Picks one metaphysical picture |
| Blocks infinite HOT regress as *complete* explanation of presence | Affirms a positive ontology (*cit*, luminous field, etc.) |

**Auditor verdict:** Step 4, if opened at all, may force only the **negative**: presence is not first acquired by becoming object for another consciousness. It does **not** uniquely entail Advaitic/Kashmiri/etc. self-manifest field. Rival labels that grant nonobjectifying presence absorb the anti-regress result without the field doctrine.

**Promotion gate L4:** **DO NOT PROMOTE on sword line.** Off-line research only; never promote because a later metaphysical destination is desired.

## 5. Recommended refined wording for Step 2’s question

### Current Notion / script wording (soft / drift risks)

> “Given the required subject–object structure, what must be true of the subject-pole such that the object appears *to* it, rather than the subject-pole itself appearing as an object in the same act?”

**Risks:**
- “What must be true of the subject-pole” invites a **substantial** answer (soul, Pure Subject, *ātman*).
- “Rather than…” can be heard as **numerical** exclusion of the organism.
- Soft on **respect / act-indexing** (“in the same act”).

### Refined question (recommended)

> **Given that** objective empirical representation requires a subject–object structure in a presenting act, **what must be true of the *role* occupied by the subject-pole in *that same act*** — such that the object appears *to* it — **without** treating that pole, *in that act and respect*, as one more object among the act’s objects?

### Refined conclusion line (pair with question)

> Whatever occupies the subject-role in a given act is not, **in that same act and respect**, the object of that act. Reflection **relocates** the boundary; it does not eliminate the need for a subject-pole. **No numerical dualism is claimed.**

Use this wording in the MAIN nest; forbid answers that smuggle entity-claims into the role-slot.
